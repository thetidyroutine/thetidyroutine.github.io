About

The Tidy Routine is about making a home that runs itself - or close to it.

No color-coded perfection, no six-hour Saturday cleans. Just short routines, smart storage and printable checklists that make the next step obvious. Everything here is written for real homes: small apartments, busy families, shared flats and anyone who would rather spend the weekend outside.
