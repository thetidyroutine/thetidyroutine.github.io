You don't need a custom closet system to have an organized wardrobe. Most closets are cluttered for two reasons: too many clothes, and space that isn't used well. Both can be fixed for very little money.

Start here: the declutter

Organizing comes second. Take everything out and sort into keep, donate and repair. A good rule: if you haven't worn it in a year and it's not seasonal or for special occasions, it goes.

The hanger trick: turn all your hangers backwards. When you wear something, hang it back the right way. After six months, you'll see exactly what you never wear.

1. Slim velvet hangers

Switching from mixed plastic and wooden hangers to slim velvet ones is the single biggest upgrade. They take up about half the space, clothes don't slip off, and a matching row looks instantly tidy.

5. Fold vertically in drawers

File-folding t-shirts (standing them up like files instead of stacking) lets you see every one at a glance. Combine with simple drawer dividers.

6. Use the door

An over-the-door shoe organizer works for much more than shoes: scarves, belts, accessories, rolled-up workout clothes.

7. Store out-of-season clothes elsewhere

Vacuum bags or under-bed boxes for off-season clothes can free up a third of your closet.

8. Hooks for tomorrow's outfit

A couple of adhesive hooks inside the closet give tomorrow's outfit and the "worn once" items a place - instead of the chair.

9. Light it up

A cheap motion-sensor LED strip in a dark closet makes a bigger difference than you'd expect. You'll actually see what you own.
