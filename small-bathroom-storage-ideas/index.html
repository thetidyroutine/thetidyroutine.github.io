10 Small Bathroom Storage Ideas for Tiny Spaces

Organization · 2026-10-19

Small bathrooms have a lot to hold: towels, toiletries, cleaning supplies, spare toilet paper, hair tools. The trick is to stop thinking about floor space and start thinking about walls, doors and the space above things.

Start by clearing out what you don't use - expired products, hotel samples and near-empty bottles. The 30-day declutter challenge has two days just for this. Then use these ideas for what's left.

1. Shelf over the toilet

The wall above the toilet is the biggest unused space in most bathrooms. A freestanding over-the-toilet unit needs no drilling and holds towels, toilet paper and baskets.

2. A shower caddy that doesn't rust

Bottles on the edge of the bath look cluttered and collect grime. A rust-proof corner or hanging caddy gets them off every surface.

3. Hooks instead of towel bars

Hooks hold more towels in less wall space than a bar, and towels dry fine on them. Put a row on the back of the door.

4. Over-the-door organizer

The back of the bathroom door can hold a full set of shelves for hair tools, spare products and cleaning supplies.

5. Drawer dividers

Bathroom drawers become a jumble fast. Small dividers give each category its own spot - makeup, dental, hair, first aid.

Drawer organizer trays

Mix-and-match trays keep small items from becoming a jumble.

6. Two-tier organizer under the sink

The same trick that works in the kitchen: a two-tier sliding organizer works around the pipes and doubles the space.

7. Rolling cart in a gap

A slim rolling cart slides between the toilet and the wall or next to the washing machine. Perfect for toilet paper and cleaning supplies.

Slim bathroom rolling cart

Fits narrow gaps beside the toilet or the washing machine.

8. Floating shelves above the door

A single shelf above the bathroom door is perfect for spare towels and backup toilet paper - things you need occasionally.

9. Magnetic strip for small metal items

A small magnetic strip inside a cabinet door holds tweezers, nail clippers and bobby pins.

10. Matching containers

Decanting hand soap and lotion into matching dispensers and using one or two baskets in the same color makes even a small, full bathroom look calm.
