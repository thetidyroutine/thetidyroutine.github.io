The Holiday Cleaning Checklist - Get Guest-Ready in 4 Weeks

Most holiday cleaning happens the night before guests arrive, in a rush, with everyone stressed. It doesn't have to. Spread the work over four weeks and each session takes less than an hour - and the week of the visit is just a quick reset.

Count back four weeks from your first gathering and start here.

Week 4 before: declutter first

Cleaning is twice as fast when there's less stuff in the way. This week is only about clearing.

  • Clear the entryway: off-season shoes and coats go to storage
  • Empty the coat closet enough to hang guests' coats
  • Clear kitchen counters to make room for cooking and serving
  • Go through the fridge and freezer - you'll need the space for holiday food
  • Make one donation bag of things you don't want guests tripping over

Week 3 before: the deep clean jobs

These are the jobs guests notice but you only do occasionally:

  • Clean inside the oven - you'll be using it a lot
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles in the kitchen
  • Wash windows in the living and dining area (daylight is low; clean glass helps)
  • Vacuum the sofa and under the cushions
  • Dust baseboards, door frames and light fixtures

Week 2 before: guest room and bathroom

If people are staying over, this week is for them.

Guest room - Wash the bedding and add an extra blanket - Clear a drawer and a few hangers - Put out a small basket with water, tissues and a phone charger - Check the bedside lamp works

Guest bathroom - Deep clean the toilet, sink and shower, including grout - Put out fresh towels and a spare toilet roll where guests can see it - Clear personal items off the counter - Refill hand soap

Week 1 before: kitchen and dining

  • Clean out the fridge one last time before the big shop
  • Wash serving dishes, glasses and table linen you haven't used all year
  • Polish cutlery if needed
  • Check you have enough chairs - borrow early if not
  • Clean the dining table and chairs, including the legs

The day before: 30-minute reset

Set a timer and do only this:

  1. Clear every flat surface in the rooms guests will see
  2. Wipe the kitchen counters and stovetop
  3. Quick clean of the guest bathroom: toilet, sink, mirror
  4. Vacuum the entry, living room and dining area
  5. Empty all bins and put fresh bags in
  6. Light a candle or open the windows for ten minutes

If you've kept up a daily routine, this is genuinely all that's left. The 15-minute evening reset keeps it that way during the visit too.

During the visit

Do a 10-minute tidy each evening: dishes in the dishwasher, counters wiped, cushions back, bins checked. It's much easier than facing a three-day mess on the last day.

After the holidays

Give yourself one session to reset: wash the guest bedding, put serving dishes away, and pack decorations neatly so next year starts easy. Label the boxes - future you will be grateful.
