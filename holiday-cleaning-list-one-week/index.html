Holiday Cleaning List - The 7-Day Plan Before Guests Arrive

By Mr. Tidy · Cleaning Routines · Nov 22, 2026

This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Learn more.

You meant to start the holiday cleaning weeks ago, and now guests arrive in seven days. Good news: a home does not need to be spotless for people to feel welcome. It needs to be clear, fresh and comfortable in the few places guests actually use.

This holiday cleaning list is the short version. It gives you one small job list per day, in an order that makes each day easier. If you have more time, the 4-week holiday cleaning checklist spreads the same work out more gently.

First, decide what guests will actually see

Walk through your home the way a guest would: front door, entryway, living room, dining area, the guest bathroom and (if they stay) the guest room. Those are your priority zones. Bedrooms with the door closed, the garage and the back of the cupboards can wait until January.

Write the zones on a sheet of paper. Everything below happens in those zones first.

Day 7: Clear

Cleaning is quick when surfaces are empty. Today you only remove things - no scrubbing yet.

  • Take a laundry basket and walk each priority room, collecting everything that lives elsewhere
  • Clear kitchen counters except the kettle, coffee machine and one fruit bowl
  • Empty the entryway: spare shoes and coats away, space made for guests' coats
  • Make one bag for donation and one for the bin

If clutter is the real problem, the 30-day declutter challenge is a good plan for January.

Day 6: Kitchen

The kitchen is where everyone ends up, so it gets its own day.

  • Clean the fridge shelves and throw out expired food - you need room for holiday shopping
  • Wipe cabinet fronts, handles and the stovetop
  • Clean the microwave (steam a bowl of water with lemon for three minutes, then wipe)
  • Wash the dishes and glasses you will serve with, especially the ones stored all year

Day 5: Bathroom

The guest bathroom is the room people remember.

  • Scrub the toilet, sink, tub or shower and wipe the mirror
  • Descale the tap and shower head if you see white buildup
  • Put out clean towels, hand soap and a spare toilet roll where it is visible
  • Clear personal items off the counter into a drawer or basket

Day 4: Guest room

Skip this if nobody is staying over.

  • Wash the sheets and make the bed with a spare blanket folded at the foot
  • Clear a drawer and a few hangers
  • Dust surfaces, vacuum, and check that the lamp and curtains work
  • Add a small tray with water, tissues and a phone charger

Day 3: Living and dining area

  • Dust shelves, the TV unit and window sills, top to bottom
  • Vacuum the sofa and under the cushions
  • Wash or shake out throws and cushion covers
  • Wipe the dining table and chairs, including the legs
  • Check you have enough chairs and glasses - borrow now, not on the day

Day 2: Floors and entryway

Floors last, because everything above drops dust down.

  • Vacuum all hard and soft floors in the priority zones
  • Mop kitchen, bathroom and entryway
  • Shake out or vacuum the doormat and set a tray or mat for wet shoes
  • Wipe the front door handle and light switches (the spots everyone touches)

Day 1: The 30-minute reset

Set a timer and do only this:

  1. Clear every flat surface in the rooms guests will see
  2. Wipe kitchen counters and the stovetop
  3. Quick bathroom wipe: toilet, sink, mirror
  4. Empty all bins and put in fresh bags
  5. Fluff the cushions and straighten throws
  6. Open the windows for ten minutes, then light a candle if you like

That is genuinely all that is left if the earlier days were done.

If you only have 3 days

Combine the days: Clear + Kitchen on day 3, Bathroom + Guest room on day 2, Living area + Floors + Reset on day 1. Drop anything that is not in a priority zone.

During the visit

Do a ten-minute tidy each evening: dishes in, counters wiped, cushions back, bins checked. The 15-minute daily reset works well for this, and it keeps the last day from becoming a three-day mess.

After guests leave

Strip the guest bed, wash the towels and put serving dishes back. Pack decorations in labelled boxes, so next year starts easy.
