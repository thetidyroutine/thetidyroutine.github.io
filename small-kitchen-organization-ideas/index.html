12 Small Kitchen Organization Ideas That Actually Add Space

Organization · 2026-10-22

A small kitchen isn't really short on space - it's short on usable space. Most cabinets are half air, the backs of doors are empty, and the area above the fridge collects dust. These twelve ideas fix that, and none needs a drill or a renovation unless you want one.

Before you buy anything, do a quick declutter: empty one cabinet at a time and only put back what you used in the last six months. Organizing less stuff is always easier.

1. Double your cabinet shelves with risers

A single shelf holding plates is mostly empty space above. A simple wire riser creates a second level for bowls or mugs.

3. Use the inside of cabinet doors

Adhesive hooks or an over-the-door rack holds measuring spoons, pot lids, cutting boards or cleaning cloths.

4. Store pot lids vertically

Lids are the most awkward things in any kitchen. A lid rack - either in a drawer or on a door - stops the avalanche.

5. File your pans like folders

Instead of stacking pans (and pulling three out to get the bottom one), stand them on their side in a pan organizer. Same for baking trays and cutting boards.

Pan organizer rack

Store pans and trays upright so any one comes out without the others.

6. Add a magnetic knife strip

A wall-mounted strip frees a whole drawer or a chunk of counter that a knife block was using.

Magnetic knife strip

7. Rail and hooks above the counter

A rail along the backsplash holds utensils, a paper towel roll and a small spice shelf. Everything you use daily is in reach and the drawers stay clear.

8. Turntables in corner cabinets

Corner cabinets swallow things. A turntable (lazy Susan) brings the back to the front with a spin. They also work brilliantly for oils and sauces.

Lazy Susan turntable

Makes deep and corner cabinets fully usable.

9. Clear stackable containers for dry goods

Decanting pasta, rice and cereal into square, stackable containers saves a surprising amount of space - round bags and boxes waste the corners. You also see when you're running low.

Airtight food storage containers

Square containers stack and waste no space; airtight keeps food fresh.

10. Use the top of the fridge

A pair of baskets on top of the fridge is perfect for things you need occasionally - baking supplies, the picnic set, spare paper towels.

11. Rolling cart in a gap

That 15 cm gap between the fridge and the wall fits a slim rolling cart - ideal for spices, oils and cans.

Slim rolling storage cart

Slides into narrow gaps next to the fridge or washing machine.

12. Keep the counter for working

The best small-kitchen rule: only things you use every day earn counter space. The toaster you use weekly can live in a cabinet. A clear counter makes a small kitchen feel twice the size.
